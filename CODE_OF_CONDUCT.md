# Code of conduct

Natterjack follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas and not people,
and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other project space.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact a maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) privately through GitHub. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be
removed from the project.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
